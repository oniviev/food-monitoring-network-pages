Methodology and indicator definitions

Each chart has its own scale. The horizontal axis shows the week-ending date; the vertical axis shows the price or indicator value.

Authors’ adaptation of the main approaches of the WFP Market Functionality Index (MFI).

* Food availability. Share of available products across the 18 monitored categories, averaged across surveyed stores.

** Food basket cost. The 17 surveyed food items are priced using fixed annual quantities for a working-age adult from the annex to Cabinet of Ministers Resolution No. 780 of 11 October 2016. Weekly quantities = annual quantities ×7/365; monthly quantities = annual quantities ÷12. The monthly basket cost is compared with the statutory subsistence minimum for a working-age adult in 2026, UAH 3.328 per month. This is the cost of the surveyed subset, not a complete diet.

*** Product variety. Share of products offered in at least three brands or types, averaged across surveyed stores.

Prices: simple averages across the surveyed chain stores, rounded to the nearest hryvnia, including VAT. Weekly changes compare the same chains with the previous collection.

The sample covers selected surveyed stores and is not representative of Kyiv as a whole.

Source: own data collected in grocery chain stores in Kyiv.

Authors: Oleh Nivievskyi, Victoria Shadrina.
