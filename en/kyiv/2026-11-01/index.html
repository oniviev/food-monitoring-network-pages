Methodology

Indicators are based on four store visits. Variety is the share of products with at least three brands or types. The basket is priced using fixed quantities of 17 food items. The sample covers the surveyed stores.

Prices: simple averages across the surveyed chain stores, rounded to the nearest hryvnia, including VAT. Weekly changes compare the same chains with the previous collection.
