Methodology

Indicators are based on two store visits. Variety is the share of products with at least three brands or types. The basket is priced using fixed quantities of 17 food items. The sample covers the surveyed stores. The current pork price was used for the previous Fozzy basket.

Prices: simple averages across the surveyed chain stores, rounded to the nearest hryvnia, including VAT. Weekly changes compare the same chains with the previous collection.
